# Computations for "Local factors of the adjoint L-function and the l-adic valuations of the modular degree"

This directory holds the scripts, result files and LaTeX tables behind the computations of the paper
*Local factors of the adjoint L-function and the l-adic valuations of the modular degree* by D. Soni, whose source is
`../paper/note.tex`. Every script is run with this directory (`final/pipeline`) as the working directory; paths such as
`data/raw`, `results/` and `tables/` are relative to it.

Origin of these files: see `PROVENANCE.md`. Software versions and known PARI issues: see `ENVIRONMENT.md`.

## Data

The input is John Cremona's `ecdata` repository (the tables of elliptic curves over Q of conductor below 500000), pinned
at commit `25cec5ecfec8b9f016eb1631ac633194c2bed39f`. `scripts/download_ecdata.sh` fetches that commit into `data/raw`
(about 1.2 GB) and records the commit in `data/raw/ECDATA_COMMIT`. The data are not committed (`.gitignore`).

## Ranges

All statements are checked on X_0(N)-optimal curves (paper, Section `sec:data`):

| range | conductors | optimal curves |
|---|---|---|
| working set | N <= 300000 | 1,311,066 |
| hold-out | 300000 < N <= 400000 | 429,936 |
| extension | 400000 < N <= 500000, restricted to the classes whose optimal curve is determined in the tables | 359,009 |
| out of table | 500000 < N <= 4,000,000, curves generated by `scripts/out_of_table.gp` with trivial isogeny class, not CM, modular degree computed with `ellmoddegree` | 893 |

### Protocol (as described in the paper, Section `sec:data`)

- The component-group inequality `eq:main` and Conjecture `conj:eis` were found on the working set, written down, and then
  tested once on the hold-out, which had been held back, and afterwards on the extension; nothing was adjusted after the
  hold-out test.
- The twist-symmetrised form `eq:twist` replaced an earlier, weaker formulation on the working set and was then tested once
  on the hold-out and the extension.
- The Euler-factor rule (Conjecture `conj:euler`, inequality `eq:rule`) was formulated on the working set from the minima
  by Kodaira type (Section `sec:iso`); the terms at 2 and 3 were fixed afterwards by PARI's Euler factors; the rule was
  tested on all four ranges; the correction term h_l(E) was identified last, from the exceptions.
- Since the hold-out and the extension had already been used for `eq:main` and `eq:twist`, the paper treats them as a
  confirmation and not a blind test of Conjecture `conj:euler`; the out-of-table curves, generated before the rule was
  formulated, are a blind test of it. They contain no curve with mod 3 image 3Ns, so the correction term h_3 was fitted on,
  and only confirmed by, data already seen. The paper does not count the out-of-table curves as a blind test of `eq:twist`,
  nor of the weights w_3.

## Quick checks (no download needed)

One curve, in PARI/GP (2.15 or later):

```
gp -q -D parisizemax=400000000 -D threadsizemax=200000000
? read("scripts/euler_rule.gp");
? rule(ellinit([1,1,1,-30,-76]), 3)     \\ 121a1
%1 = [1, 1]
```

`rule(E, l)` returns `[v_l(deg phi_E), bound]` with the type III terms left out; `rule(E, l, 1)` includes them and the
correction term at l = 3. E must be the optimal curve of its class with E[l] irreducible (paper, appendix `app:repro`).

Self-test (Python with pandas and cypari2; `pip install -r requirements.txt`):

```
python3 scripts/selftest.py | tee results/selftest.log
```

It (a) loads `scripts/euler_rule.gp` through cypari2 and evaluates it on 121a1, 539c1, 539d1, 1232l1, 592c1, 726a1,
990h1, 990h3 and 11a1 against the values and degrees stated in the paper, and checks PARI's symmetric-square Euler factor
at 2 for 336f1, 1232l1 and 592c1 against the polynomials in the paper; (b) checks every printed table in `tables/`
against the committed result files (the four twisted rows of `isolated3.tex` excepted, see below); (c) checks that each
table in `tables/` appears verbatim in `../paper/note.tex`. The committed `results/selftest.log` is its output with PARI 2.17.2.

## Full reproduction

Requirements: PARI/GP with the `gp` binary, Python 3.11 or later with `requirements.txt`, bash, curl.

```
bash scripts/repro_run.sh         # stage 1: data, PARI pass, feature table, optimal curves, checker
bash scripts/reproduce_tables.sh  # stage 2: Euler factors, the rule, the six printed tables
```

`scripts/repro_run.sh` downloads the pinned data (files already present are kept), runs `scripts/pari_features.gp` on
every allcurves file (a gp error is fatal), builds `data/curves.parquet` and `data/ap.parquet`
(`scripts/build_features.py`), writes `results/optimal_{work,holdout,ext}.parquet` and runs `scripts/conj_check.py` on
each range. It checks the row counts after every stage (3,064,705 curves, 2,164,260 isogeny classes, 1,311,066 / 429,936 /
359,009 optimal curves) and compares the regenerated `results/check_{work,holdout,ext}_summary.csv` with the committed ones;
it exits nonzero on any failure, count mismatch or difference.

`scripts/reproduce_tables.sh` then runs, in this order: `oot_convert.py` (from the committed `results/oot_{1,2}.tsv`),
`conj_check.py` on the out-of-table curves, `step10_sym2_euler.py` and `step10_rule.py` on the four ranges,
`step11_tw_boundary.py`, `step12_tamagawa.py work`, `step6_local.py`, `step6_u1_fit.py work`, `step7_twist2.py` on the three
in-table ranges, `step9_euler.py 3` and `make_tables.py`; it then compares every regenerated summary and table with the
committed version, runs `scripts/selftest.py`, and exits nonzero on any difference. The out-of-table curves themselves are
not regenerated: `scripts/out_of_table.gp` draws random curves under a time budget (usage in its header; the logs of the
runs behind `results/oot_*.tsv` are `results/oot_*.log`).

Both scripts write their outputs in place in `results/` and `tables/` (as the individual scripts do), after copying the
committed versions to `logs/<script>_<UTC time>/ref/`; the run log is in the same directory (`logs/` is git-ignored).
After a run, `sha256sum -c MANIFEST.sha256` lists the files that changed (logs with timestamps always do).

After the download, stage 1 takes about half an hour and stage 2 a few hours (`ENVIRONMENT.md`).

## Printed tables

The paper prints its tables inline; the files in `tables/` hold the same tabular bodies (checked by `scripts/selftest.py`).

| paper label | content (from the caption) | file | written by | reads |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tab:uniform` | Conjecture `conj:euler` with PARI's factors at all primes, four ranges, l = 3, 5, 7, 11, 13 | `tables/euler_uniform.tex` | `scripts/step11_tw_boundary.py` | `results/step10_rule_<set>_l<l>.parquet` (`step10_rule.py`), `results/sym2_<set>_*.parquet` (`step10_sym2_euler.py`), `results/optimal_<set>.parquet`, `results/oot_table.parquet`, `data/curves.parquet` (mod 3 image labels, a-invariants); the same numbers are in `results/step11_summary.csv` |
| `tab:iso` | working set, l = 3: minimum of v_3(deg phi) by additive type and Euler term e | `tables/euler_iso3.tex` | transcribed by hand from `results/step9_euler_l3.txt`, which `scripts/step9_euler.py 3` writes | `step9_euler.py` reads `results/step7_twist2_work_l3.parquet` (`step7_twist2.py work`), `results/local_primes.parquet` (`step6_local.py`), `data/curves.parquet` |
| `tab:counts` | verification counts for the corollary forms `eq:main`, `eq:twist` and Conjecture `conj:eis` | `tables/counts.tex` | `scripts/make_tables.py` | `results/check_{work,holdout,ext,oot}_summary.csv` (`conj_check.py`) |
| `tab:ns` | the curves with mod 3 image 3Ns | `tables/ns3.tex` | `scripts/step11_tw_boundary.py` | as for `tab:uniform`; the same numbers are in `results/step11_ns3.csv` |
| `tab:isolated` | working set, l = 3: minimum of v_3(deg phi) over curves whose only contributions come from the named primes | `tables/isolated3.tex` | `scripts/make_tables.py` | untwisted rows: `results/step6_u1_fit_work.txt` (stdout of `step6_u1_fit.py work`, which reads `results/local_primes.parquet` and `data/curves.parquet`); twisted rows: `results/step7_twist2_work_l3.parquet` (`step7_twist2.py work`) |
| `tab:oot` | the out-of-table curves by family | `tables/oot.tex` | `scripts/make_tables.py` | `results/oot_table.parquet` (`oot_convert.py`, from `results/oot_{1,2}.tsv`) |

`make_tables.py` writes all four of its tables in one run and needs all of its inputs, including
`results/step7_twist2_<set>_l<l>.parquet`. `scripts/step10_make_table.py` writes an older layout to
`tables/euler_uniform.tex`; the printed layout is the one from `step11_tw_boundary.py`.

Not produced here: Table `tab:excess` of the paper (conductor below 10,000) is not written by any script in this directory.
Not printed: `tables/twist.tex` (written by `make_tables.py`) and `tables/euler_full.tex` (no script here writes it; its
counts come from an earlier run of the step 9 rule and differ from `results/step9_fullrule_*_summary.csv`).

### Inputs that are not committed

All `*.parquet` files (`data/*.parquet`, `results/*.parquet`) are git-ignored and rebuilt by the scripts above:
`data/curves.parquet`, `data/ap.parquet`, `results/optimal_<set>.parquet`, `results/check_<set>_perl.parquet`,
`results/oot_table.parquet`, `results/sym2_<set>_<shard>.parquet`, `results/step10_rule_<set>_l<l>.parquet`,
`results/local_primes.parquet`, `results/step7_twist2_<set>_l<l>.parquet` and the per-curve files of the exploratory steps.
The committed summaries cover every printed number except the four twisted rows of `tables/isolated3.tex` (II, II* and
I_n* at odd q and at q = 2), which are computed directly from `results/step7_twist2_work_l3.parquet` and therefore cannot
be re-derived from committed files alone; they need stage 1 and `step7_twist2.py work`.

## results/

Grouped by step; "set" is one of work, holdout, ext, oot.

Checker of the corollary forms (`conj_check.py`)
- `check_{work,holdout,ext,oot}_summary.csv`: per prime l, curves, positive bounds and violations of `eq:main`, `eq:twist`, `conj:eis` (Table `tab:counts`).

Reproducibility runs of stage 1 (older version of `repro_run.sh`, 2026-10-06)
- `repro.log`: first run on a clean clone; download, PARI pass, feature table and optimal-curve counts completed (3,064,705 curves; 1,311,066 / 429,936 / 359,009), but the checker step failed because the clone predated `scripts/conj_check.py`, so no summaries were compared.
- `repro_check.log`: checker rerun with the clone's version of `conj_check.py`, whose summary had fewer columns; the script reported DIFFERS for that reason, and the columns common to both versions agree in every row shown.
- `repro_check2.log`: checker rerun with the current `conj_check.py`; the three summaries were identical to the committed ones.

Step 5 (pre-registered search of the exploratory stage; `step5.py`, `step5_cells.py`, `step5_report.py`)
- `step5_results.csv`, `step5_local.csv`, `step5_log.txt`: nested-model tests with permutation null, local gains, log.
- `step5_controls.csv`, `step5_controls_local.csv`, `controls_stdout.txt`: the control tests.
- `candidates_stdout.txt`: console output of the candidate tests.
- `step5_cells.csv`, `step5_cells_pairs.csv`, `cells_stdout.txt`: forbidden, depleted and enriched cells.
- `STEP5_REPORT.md`: report assembled by `step5_report.py` from these files.

Step 6 (modular degree against local data; `step6_*.py`)
- `step6_local.txt`: stdout of `step6_local.py` (bad-prime rows per set and Kodaira type counts).
- `step6_u1_work.txt`, `step6_u1b_{work,holdout}.txt`, `step6_u1c_{work,holdout}.txt`: the three passes of candidate U1 (Tamagawa numbers, Tamagawa exponents, multiplicative primes).
- `step6_u1_fit_{work,holdout}.txt`: isolated configurations by additive type and the unified component-group inequality; `step6_u1_fit_work.txt` feeds the untwisted rows of Table `tab:isolated`.
- `step6_u1_add_holdout.txt`, `step6_u1_twist_holdout.txt`: additive primes and twisted contributions at l = 3 on the hold-out.

Step 7 (twisted primes, equality cases, mechanism tests)
- `step7_twist2_{work,holdout,ext}.txt`: stdout of `step7_twist2.py` (twisted primes with q = 2 separated).
- `step7_equality_work.txt`, `step7_equality2_work.txt`: equality cases of the bounds.
- `step7_twist_test.txt`: test of a congruence of f with its own quadratic twist.

Step 9 (Euler-factor terms by Kodaira type, q >= 5)
- `step9_euler_l{3,5,7}.txt`, `step9_euler_l5l7.log`: single-additive-prime minima (`step9_euler.py`); `step9_euler_l3.txt` is the source of Table `tab:iso`.
- `step9_fullrule_{work,holdout,ext,oot}_summary.csv`, `step9_fullrule_all.log`, `step9_fullrule_others.log`: the step 9 form of the rule (`step9_fullrule.py`).
- `step9_twist_degree_work.csv`: violations of twist monotonicity of the degree (`step9_twist_degree.py`; header only, none found).

Step 10 (symmetric-square Euler factors and the rule)
- `sym2_{work_0,work_1,work_2,holdout,ext,oot}.log`: logs of `step10_sym2_euler.py` (curves and rows per shard).
- `step10_rule_{work,holdout,ext,oot}_summary.csv`, `step10_rule.log`: the rule and its variants per range (`step10_rule.py`).

Step 11 (correction term at l = 3)
- `step11_summary.csv`, `step11_ns3.csv`, `step11.log`: Tables `tab:uniform` and `tab:ns` (`step11_tw_boundary.py`).

Step 12 (Tamagawa factors of the adjoint)
- `step12_work.csv`, `step12_work.log`: the strengthened inequality of Section `sec:tam` on the working set (`step12_tamagawa.py work`).

Out of table
- `oot_1.tsv`, `oot_2.tsv`: generated curves with their modular degrees (`out_of_table.gp`, modes 1 and 2).
- `oot_1.log`, `oot_1b.log`, `oot_1c.log`, `oot_2.log`, `oot_2c.log`, `oot_2d.log`, `oot_2e.log`: gp logs of the generator runs behind them.

Other checks
- `twist_lemma_check.txt`: output of `twist_lemma_check.gp` (Kodaira types under quadratic twist, PARI 2.15.4).
- `selftest.log`: output of `scripts/selftest.py`.
