# Line-up for Chapter 1: Groups, group actions and matrix groups over finite fields

Label `ch:groups`, file `chapters/ch01_groups.tex`, Part I (Algebra). Target length 1900 lines (card excluded).

This chapter is the first of the book: it relies on no earlier chapter, only on the assumed background
(linear algebra over R and C, elementary group theory as in RHB Chapters 28 and 29). Every result is
elementary; everything is proved here except Tychonoff's theorem, the topological characterisation of
profinite groups, the isomorphisms PGL_2(F_5) = S_5 and the simplicity of PSL_2(F_7), the classification of
groups of order 8, and Dickson's classification, which are stated with references.

Conventions fixed for the whole chapter (and inherited by the book):

* C1. Matrices act on column vectors on the left. For $v \in F^2 \setminus \{0\}$ the line through $v$ is
  $[v] = Fv$; $\mathbb{P}^1(F)$ is the set of lines of $F^2$, with coordinates $[x:y]$; we write
  $\infty = [1:0]$ and identify $[x:1]$ with $x \in F$, so that $g = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}$
  acts by the Moebius formula $g \cdot x = (ax+b)/(cx+d)$ (check: $g[x:1] = [ax+b : cx+d]$).
* C2. Conjugation is $g \cdot h = ghg^{-1}$. In a semidirect product $N \rtimes H$ the group $H$ acts on $N$ and
  $hnh^{-1} = \theta(h)(n)$.
* C3. $C_n$ or $\mathbb{Z}/n$ is the cyclic group of order $n$; $D_n$ is the dihedral group of order $2n$ (so
  $D_4$ has order 8; some authors write $D_8$); $V_4 = C_2 \times C_2$; $Q_8$ the quaternion group; $SD_{16}$
  the semidihedral group of order 16.
* C4. $\mathbb{F}_p = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$ is a field (an integer prime to $p$ is invertible modulo $p$ by Bezout).
  $\mathbb{F}_q$ is a finite field with $q$ elements, constructed in Chapter 2; in this chapter $q$ is odd
  whenever Cartan subgroups are discussed, and the reader may take $q = p$ prime throughout. Warning: in this
  chapter $q$ is a field size; in the paper $q$ is a bad prime of $E$ (the two agree in the paper's
  $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3)$, where the field size is $\ell = 3$).
* C5. "The image is $N_s(\mathbb{F}_3)$" always means "conjugate in $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3)$ to $N_s(\mathbb{F}_3)$";
  the standard models of the subgroups (Section 6) are fixed once and for all.

## Section 1. Groups, subgroups, cosets, quotients and cyclic groups

**1.1 `def:groups:group` (definition, background).** A group, abelian group, subgroup, the subgroup $\langle S \rangle$
generated by a subset, the order $\#G$ of a group and the order of an element, cyclic groups $\langle g \rangle$;
examples: $(\mathbb{Z},+)$, $\mathbb{Z}/n$, $S_n$, $\mathrm{GL}_2(F)$, the group of units $F^\times$ of a field.
Reference: Dummit-Foote (DF) Chapter 1. Paper use: background.

**1.2 `prop:groups:lagrange` (proposition, PH).** Let $H \le G$ be groups with $G$ finite. The left cosets $gH$
partition $G$, all have $\#H$ elements, and $\#G = [G:H] \cdot \#H$. Consequently the order of every element
divides $\#G$, and $g^{\#G} = 1$ for all $g \in G$. Reference: DF Section 3.2. Paper use: background
(orders of the subgroups of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3)$).

**1.3 `thm:groups:cyclic` (theorem, PH).** Let $C = \langle t \rangle$ be cyclic of order $n$.
(i) Every subgroup of $C$ is cyclic; for each $d \mid n$ there is exactly one subgroup of order $d$, namely
$C[d] = \{x : x^d = 1\} = \langle t^{n/d} \rangle = C^{n/d}$ (the $(n/d)$-th powers).
(ii) For $m \ge 1$ the $m$-th power map $x \mapsto x^m$ is an endomorphism of $C$ with kernel $C[\gcd(m,n)]$ of
order $\gcd(m,n)$ and image $C^m = C^{\gcd(m,n)}$ of index $\gcd(m,n)$; in particular for a prime $\ell$ and
$k \ge 0$, $[C : C^{\ell^k}] = \ell^{\min(k, v_\ell(n))}$, and $x \in C$ is an $\ell^k$-th power iff its class in
$C/C^{\ell^{v_\ell(n)}} \simeq \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{v_\ell(n)}$ is divisible by $\ell^k$ (for $k \le v_\ell(n)$).
(iii) The generators of $C$ are the $\varphi(n)$ elements of order $n$; $\mathrm{Aut}(C) = (\mathbb{Z}/n)^\times$ acting by
$t \mapsto t^a$; $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}) = \{\pm 1\}$. (iv) A homomorphism $\chi\colon G \to C$ from any group onto $C$ satisfies
$\chi^2 = 1$ (i.e. $\chi(g)^2 = 1$ for all $g$) iff $n \mid 2$. Reference: DF Section 2.3. Paper use:
Lemma lem:tamfactor (the class $c$ of the unit part of the Tate parameter in $\mathbb{F}_q^\times/(\mathbb{F}_q^\times)^{\ell^{v_\ell(q-1)}}$,
restated for $\mathbb{F}_q^\times$ in Chapter 2), Lemma lem:crit ($\mathbb{T}/(I+J)$ cyclic of order $r$), Lemma lem:h0
($\bar\chi_\ell$ surjects onto $\mathbb{F}_\ell^\times$, cyclic of order $\ell - 1$, so $\bar\chi_\ell^2 = 1$ forces $\ell - 1 \mid 2$, i.e. $\ell = 3$).

**1.4 `def:groups:normal` (definition, background).** Normal subgroup, quotient group $G/N$, homomorphism, kernel, image,
index, the canonical projection; simple group; the centre $Z(G)$, the centraliser $C_G(S)$ and the normaliser
$N_G(S)$ of a subset; conjugacy classes; the commutator subgroup $[G,G]$ and the abelianisation. Reference:
DF Sections 2.2, 3.1. Paper use: background ($N_s$, $N_{ns}$ are normalisers; $\{\pm I\}$ is the centre of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3)$).

**1.5 `thm:groups:isothms` (theorem, PH).** (First isomorphism theorem) a homomorphism $f\colon G \to H$ induces
$G/\ker f \simeq f(G)$. (Second) for $H \le G$ and $N \trianglelefteq G$, $HN/N \simeq H/(H \cap N)$. (Third) for
$N \le M$ both normal in $G$, $(G/N)/(M/N) \simeq G/M$. (Correspondence) subgroups of $G/N$ correspond bijectively
and inclusion-preservingly to subgroups of $G$ containing $N$, normal ones to normal ones. Reference: DF
Section 3.3. Paper use: background.

**1.6 `lem:groups:index2` (lemma, PH).** (i) A subgroup of index 2 is normal, and is the kernel of a surjection
onto $C_2$. (ii) (Image of a kernel.) Let $\rho\colon G \to \Gamma$ and $\delta\colon \Gamma \to A$ be homomorphisms
and $G_0 = \ker(\delta \circ \rho)$. Then $\rho(G_0) = \rho(G) \cap \ker \delta$. In particular, if $\det \rho\colon G \to \{\pm1\}$
has kernel $G_0$, the image of $G_0$ is the determinant-one subgroup $\rho(G) \cap \mathrm{SL}_2$ of the image of $G$.
Reference: elementary. Paper use: Proposition prop:ns, first sentence of the proof (the image of
$\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3}))$ is the determinant-one subgroup of the image $H$ of $\bar\rho_{E,3}$,
because $\det \bar\rho_{E,3} = \chi_{-3}$ has kernel $\mathrm{Gal}(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}/\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt{-3}))$).

**1.7 `prop:groups:conjclasses` (proposition, PH).** Conjugacy is an equivalence relation; the class of $g$ has
$[G : C_G(g)]$ elements; a subgroup is normal iff it is a union of conjugacy classes; the number of conjugates of
a subgroup $H$ is $[G : N_G(H)]$. Reference: DF Section 4.3. Paper use: the class table of $\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{F}_3)$ (6.1).

**1.8 `prop:groups:lprimary` (proposition, PH).** Let $A$ be a finite abelian group and $\ell$ a prime. The
$\ell$-primary part $A[\ell^\infty] = \{a : \ell^k a = 0 \text{ for some } k\}$ is a subgroup of order
$\ell^{v_\ell(\#A)}$, $A = \bigoplus_\ell A[\ell^\infty]$ (sum over the primes dividing $\#A$), and $A[\ell^\infty]$
is the unique Sylow $\ell$-subgroup of $A$ and the image of the idempotent projector; $v_\ell(\#A) = v_\ell(\#A[\ell^\infty])$.
(Foretaste: $A[\ell^\infty] = A \otimes \mathbb{Z}_\ell$, Chapter 3.) The exponent of $A$ divides $\#A$ and has the same
prime divisors. Reference: DF Section 5.2. Paper use: Definition def:terms and Section 1.4 of the paper
("the $\ell$-part of the Tamagawa number", $v_\ell(\#\Phi^{\mathrm{geom}}_q)$), Lemma lem:tamfactor ($H^1(I_q,M)_{\mathrm{tor}}$
as a finite abelian $\ell$-group, "the $\ell$-part of $c_q(E)$").
